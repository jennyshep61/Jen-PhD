MODIS-HLS SG-STL processing

1. MODIS observations were aggregated to monthly plot means; HLS monthly observations were retained.
2. Sites were matched using site_crosswalk.csv and aligned to the MODIS master observed mortality year.
3. The analysis window was restricted to four years before through five years after mortality and to dates shared by both sensor records.
4. Missing months were linearly interpolated only to create regular monthly series for filtering/decomposition.
5. A Savitzky-Golay filter (7-month window, second-order polynomial) was applied.
6. Robust STL decomposition used a 12-month period and seasonal window 13.
7. STL trends were centred on their mean across months -24 to -1.
8. Eligibility required >=60 common-window months, <=20% missing observations for each sensor, and >=12 observed pre-event months for each sensor.
